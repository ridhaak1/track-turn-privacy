Track Turn

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

The short version

Track Turn is a puzzle game for Android made by an independent developer (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what happens with data when you play. I do not collect any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to me.

1Data stored on your phone

Your progress (current and unlocked levels), your best stars per level, which tutorial hints you have seen, and your settings (vibration, sounds, appearance) are saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. The game never sends them anywhere.

If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include them in your phone's backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps; that backup is controlled by you and Google, and I never receive it.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them from the phone. You can also reset your progress at any time in the game's Settings.

2Content updates (Expo / EAS Update)

To deliver fixes and new levels without a full app update, the game uses Expo's update service (EAS Update), run by Expo (650 Industries, Inc.). When the game starts, your phone asks Expo's servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection whether a newer version of the game content exists, and downloads it in the background if so. The game works fully offline; if there is no connection, it simply skips the check.

According to Expo's documentation and privacy policy, this request contains only technical information needed to deliver the right update, and no identifying information such as unique device identifiers. It includes:

  • the phone's operating system (Android);
  • the game's project ID, update channel and version information (runtime version, the ID of the update currently installed and of the one that came with the app, and IDs of recently failed updates);
  • only if the game crashed the last time it ran: the technical error message of that crash (so a broken update can be rolled back). It contains no personal information;
  • a random token created by the app when it is installed, used by Expo to tell whether an installation has requested an update. It is not linked to you, your account or your phone's hardware IDs, and it is removed from the phone when you uninstall the game.

Like any internet request, it also reveals your network's IP address to Expo's servers. I do not receive or use this information. Expo processes it under its own privacy policy (see also Expo's privacy explained page).

3Permissions

The game asks for no permissions while you play (no pop-ups). Android grants these basic permissions at install time:

  • Internet INTERNET – to check for and download content updates.
  • View network connections ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – so the update check knows whether a connection is available.
  • Control vibration VIBRATE – for the small haptic taps in the game (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Change audio settings MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS – used by the sound-effects player for audio playback. The game never records audio and has no microphone access.

4Children

Track Turn is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.
